# Commercial framing

## Product concept

CareLoop is a coordination layer for organizations that need to make
screening follow-up visible and accountable across navigators, clinicians,
program managers, and patients. The current code demonstrates case ownership,
stall escalation, handoff, evidence-backed closure, audit, aggregate privacy
suppression, and deterministic patient communication templates.

## Potential users

Potential users include screening programs, primary-care networks, specialty
referral programs, diagnostic services, and care-management teams. These are
target segments to validate, not current customers or signed commitments.

## Potential value hypothesis

The hypothesis is that explicit ownership, stage visibility, and human-reviewed
communication could reduce avoidable coordination gaps. The simulator can
explore conditional operational scenarios, but it does not quantify savings or
clinical improvement. Any dollar value, adoption rate, market size, or return
on investment would be **ASSUMPTION** until supported by customer discovery and
observed data.

## Possible delivery model

An eventual offering could be an organization-hosted or approved cloud service
with integration, role administration, monitoring, audit retention, and support.
The current repository is a local prototype with SQLite and no production
identity, messaging, EHR, security, or compliance implementation. No pricing,
contract, SLA, or revenue claim is made here.

## Adoption gates

Commercial readiness requires validated workflow value, security and privacy
controls, clinical governance, accessibility and language quality, integration
contracts, support ownership, deployment/recovery procedures, and a clear
responsibility model for human review. Synthetic demo success is not a release
criterion by itself.
